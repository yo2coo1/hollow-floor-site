Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Hollow Floor ("we") runs an automated publishing tool, the Hollow Floor Uploader, that posts our own original videos to our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. This policy explains what that tool accesses.

What the tool accesses

  • Our own accounts only. The tool signs in to the Hollow Floor accounts with the platforms' official APIs (YouTube Data API, TikTok Content Posting API, Instagram Graph API) to upload our videos, set titles, captions and thumbnails, and read our own videos' public statistics.
  • Public comments on our videos. To run the viewer vote, the tool reads public comments on our own Part 1 videos and counts which of two choices each commenter named. Only the totals are stored. Comment text and commenter identifiers are processed in memory to count one vote per person and are not stored, sold or shared.

What we do not do

  • We do not access anyone else's account, private messages or personal data.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and we do not use data for advertising.
  • We do not use Google user data to train AI models.

Google / YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By watching or interacting with our videos you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are stored encrypted (as encrypted secrets of our private automation) and used only by the tool. We store only: the IDs and links of our own uploaded videos, our own videos' statistics (views, likes, watch time) and vote totals. Rendered video files are deleted within 5 days.

Refresh and deletion of YouTube API data. Statistics we receive from the YouTube API are refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days, as the YouTube API Services Developer Policies require. If access is revoked, all data received from the YouTube API is deleted within 7 days.

How to revoke access or request deletion. The only Google account connected to this tool is the Hollow Floor channel's owner. That access can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page (third-party connections). Anyone can ask us to delete data relating to them (for example a vote counted from their comment) by contacting us as described below; we act within 7 days.
